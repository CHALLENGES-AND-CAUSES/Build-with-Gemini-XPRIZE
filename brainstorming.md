# Build with Gemini XPRIZE - Brainstorming

The goal is to find a business that:
1. Solves a problem for at least 100,000 people.
2. Has a clear path to paying customers (revenue) within 90 days.
3. Is run fundamentally by AI (e.g. Gemini Flash doing multimodal analysis or Gemma running locally).

## Idea 1: The Proactive Inventory Agent (Small Business Services)
**The Hook:** "An AI that watches your restaurant's sales and automatically calls suppliers to re-order ingredients before you run out."
**The AI:** Connects to a POS system (Square/Toast), uses Gemini Flash to predict ingredient depletion, and uses a voice agent to call suppliers.
**Business Viability:** Restaurants lose thousands to food waste and stockouts. They would easily pay $50-$100/mo for a reliable automated inventory manager.

## Idea 2: The "Over-the-Shoulder" AI Tutor (Education & Human Potential)
**The Hook:** "A tutor that watches your child's screen while they study and proactively jumps in to help exactly when they get stuck."
**The AI:** A desktop app using Gemma (running locally for privacy) that analyzes the screen at 1 frame per second. When it detects frustration or stalling on a math problem, it pops up to offer Socratic guidance.
**Business Viability:** Parents spend hundreds on tutors. Charging $20/mo for an always-on, completely private local AI tutor is highly compelling.

## Idea 3: The 24/7 Intake Paralegal (Professional Services Access)
**The Hook:** "An AI paralegal that instantly calls every lead that hits your website, qualifies their case, and books them on your calendar while you sleep."
**The AI:** When a web form is submitted, a voice agent calls the lead within 30 seconds. It uses Gemini Flash to parse their verbal case details and determine if they are a good fit for the law firm.
**Business Viability:** Law firms miss out on massive retainers when they don't respond to leads fast enough. Charging $150/mo for a 24/7 intake agent is a no-brainer for small law firms.

## Idea 4: The Grant Writer Copilot (Entrepreneurship & Job Creation)
**The Hook:** "An AI that reads your startup's entire codebase and business plan, and automatically drafts bespoke applications for government grants."
**The AI:** Uses Gemini 1.5 Pro's massive context window to ingest entire repositories and business documents, outputting highly specific grant proposals.
**Business Viability:** Non-dilutive funding is a massive pain point for founders. Charging $100 per generated grant proposal (or a monthly subscription) provides immediate ROI.

## Idea 5: The Medical Bill Negotiator (Money & Financial Access)
**The Hook:** "An AI that reads your confusing medical bills, checks them against hospital pricing databases, and automatically calls the billing department to negotiate them down."
**The AI:** Uses Gemini Flash's vision capabilities to extract line items from photos of medical bills, cross-references them, and drafts negotiation letters/scripts.
**Business Viability:** Medical debt is a huge problem. You can charge a flat fee of $15 or take a percentage of the money saved.
