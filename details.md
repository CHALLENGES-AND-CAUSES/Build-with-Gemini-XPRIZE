# Build with Gemini XPRIZE

The **Build with Gemini XPRIZE** is a global hackathon and venture challenge launched by XPRIZE in collaboration with Google. It's designed to identify and fund actual, operational AI-native businesses using the Gemini AI stack. The goal is to build an AI business that solves a socially impactful problem at scale—specifically, something that impacts **100,000 people or more**.

## Key Details
* **Objective:** Participants have 90 days to ideate, build, launch, and grow a real business that uses Google’s Gemini AI stack to solve genuine market problems.
* **Core Requirements:** Projects must move beyond concepts to demonstrate real production users and measurable revenue.
* **Scale Requirement:** Aim to solve a problem that impacts at least 100,000 people.
* **Total Prize Pool:** $2,000,000 USD across 25 winners.
* **Timeline:**
  * **Submission Period:** May 19, 2026 – August 17, 2026.
  * **Deadline:** August 18, 2026 @ 1:30am GMT+5:30.
  * **Judging Period:** August 18, 2026 – September 15, 2026.
  * **Moonshot Gathering (Finalist Pitch):** September 25, 2026, in Los Angeles. Five finalists will present to a panel of judges and an audience of 1,500 builders.
* **Website:** https://www.geminixprize.com/ / https://xprize.devpost.com/

## Competition Categories
Projects must align with one of the following five focus areas spanning economic opportunity and professional access:
1. **Education & Human Potential:** Transforming how we learn, grow, and achieve our best.
2. **Entrepreneurship & Job Creation:** Fueling the tools that help new founders and economies thrive.
3. **Small Business Services:** Powering everyday businesses with tools to compete and win.
4. **Money & Financial Access:** Breaking down barriers to banking, capital, and financial freedom.
5. **Professional Services Access:** Connecting everyday people with the expert guidance they need.

## The Approach: Prompt to Startup
* **Natural Language Planning:** You describe how the business runs in natural language—including the product, pricing, and marketing.
* **AI Collaboration:** AI agents help you build the product and work alongside you, empowering you to steer the business day-to-day. 

## What to Submit
* **GitHub repo:** Shared with testing@devpost.com and judging@hacker.fund (Public or Private).
* **3-minute video:** Demonstrating the extent to which AI is live in production and executes key decisions.
* **Written narrative:** (500–1000 words) describing how the team uses AI day to day, what humans do vs what AI does, the jobs and economic opportunities created, and the story of building the business.
* **Revenue evidence:** E.g. Stripe dashboard export or bank statement and simple P&L. Include corporate ID if available.
* **Expenses incurred:** Expenses during the hackathon period within your P&L, including marketing and customer acquisition spend (even if zero).
* **Product evidence:** Agent execution logs, API usage records, screenshots of dashboards, etc., showing AI is running continuously in production.
* **Customer evidence:** Contact info of real customers and testimonials/feedback.

## Eligibility
* The competition is open globally to individuals (above legal age of majority in country of residence), teams, and small organizations (fewer than 25 employees).
* Certain regions are excluded based on U.S. regulations.

## Technical Mandate
Participants are required to use Google's AI stack. **Your business has to be operated by AI agents and must use at least one product from Google Cloud**, including the Gemini API and Vertex AI, for at least one large language model (LLM) function within your solution. Google provides credits to help get you started.

## Judging Criteria
Submissions are evaluated on three equally weighted criteria:
1. **Business Viability:** Teams must launch a real business during the hackathon, acquire real users, and generate real revenue. Judges assess actual revenue achieved during the 90-day window and sustainability of the business model.
2. **AI-Native Operations:** Teams must run their business through AI. Judges assess the extent to which AI is live in production and executes key decisions.
3. **Category Impact:** Teams built something that meaningfully moves the needle in their chosen category — either by redefining how something works at a fundamental level, or by reaching a scale where widespread adoption is credible.

## The 4 Phases
1. **Ideate**: Pick a real problem worth solving. AI helps you scope it.
2. **Build**: Build a working prototype using AI.
3. **Ship**: Deploy a business that operates with AI. Real users in production.
4. **Sell**: Market it. Grow it. Show the revenue.
