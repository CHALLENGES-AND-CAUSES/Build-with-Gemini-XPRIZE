# Build with Gemini XPRIZE FAQ

This document covers frequently asked questions regarding eligibility, resources, project requirements, judging, and submission.

## Eligibility
* **Age Requirement:** Participants must be above the legal age of majority in their country of residence. Minors can participate but are NOT eligible for prizes.
* **Location/Citizenship:** Eligibility is based on the country of legal residence. See official rules for prohibited countries.
* **Students/Solo Participants:** Allowed. You can participate as an individual.
* **Multiple Teams/Projects:** You can join multiple teams or submit multiple projects, but each submission must be unique and substantially different.
* **Team Size:** No maximum limit, but the team must appoint one representative to receive and distribute prizes.

## Credits & Resources
* **Google Cloud & Antigravity:** Free tiers and getting started resources are available at https://cloud.google.com/free and https://antigravity.google/ ($0/month plan). 
* **Extra Credits:** A limited number of 3-month Google Ultra Plan coupons are available for builders who struggle to afford resources. Applications undergo review.

## Project Requirements
* **Business vs. Entity:** A project is a "business" (activity providing goods/services for payment) that operates with AI. A "business entity" is the legal structure. You do NOT need a newly created legal entity; existing ones can be used, but the *business/project* itself must be new.
* **Newly Created:** The business you are building must be created *after* May 19, 2026. Pre-existing templates/code are allowed if you explain their use.
* **Platform:** Must use at least one Google Cloud product (e.g., Gemini API via AI Studio or Vertex AI).
* **AI-Operated:** AI must transform workflows in the business and execute key decisions.
* **Existing Audiences/Employees:** You may use existing social media audiences or employees, but you must attest that work didn't start before the hackathon and outline associated employee expenses. Existing customer revenue must be reported separately.
* **Target Audience/Niche:** You are allowed and encouraged to target a specific niche within a category (e.g., building a product just for restaurants in the Small Business Services track). Finding a specific market often aids business viability.

## Judging
* **Criteria:** Business Viability, AI-Native Operations, and Category Impact (all equally weighted).
* **Revenue/Customers:** There is no specific minimum revenue or customer threshold, and $0 revenue in a month does not automatically disqualify you, but business viability is a core judging criterion.
* **Nonprofits:** If you structure your project as a nonprofit, your revenue must still be *earned revenue* (selling a product/service), not *contributed revenue* (donations, grants, sponsorships). The business must be viable.
* **Confidentiality:** Judges adhere to confidentiality and NDA. Private repos are shared securely. You can anonymize Personally Identifiable Information (PII) if needed.

## Submission
* **Revenue Reporting:** Earned revenue is requested, but the form provides space for context.
* **Expenses:** Report all expenses associated with the business during the hackathon (including salary, marketing, customer acquisition).
* **Submission Process:** You must submit through Devpost (xprize.devpost.com). Emailing a GitHub repo does not count.
* **GitHub Repos:** Can be owned by an individual or a company organization. Must contain all source code and be shared with `testing@devpost.com` and `judging@hacker.fund` if private.
* **Language:** All submission materials must be in English or have English translations.
