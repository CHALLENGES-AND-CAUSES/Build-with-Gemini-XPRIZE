# Marketing & Go-To-Market Strategy
*(Insights from "How to Actually Take Software to Market in 2026" with Farza Majeed)*

Since the **Build with Gemini XPRIZE** heavily weighs "Business Viability" (real users, real revenue in 90 days), getting your product to market quickly is arguably more important than perfect code. 

Here is the distilled strategy for launching and growing your AI business during the hackathon:

## 1. The "One-Liner" Hook
* **Find your "First Frame" before coding:** Don't start building until you have a compelling one-liner. It should be an idea that instantly stops someone from scrolling. (e.g., *"An AI that can see your screen and teach you anything"*).
* **If it doesn't sound exciting as an Instagram caption, don't build it yet.** You must be a fan of the messaging yourself.
* **Build and market simultaneously:** Don't separate "build weeks" from "marketing weeks." Whenever you build a feature, simultaneously figure out how you are going to announce it.

## 2. Getting Your First 10-50 Customers
* **Avoid social media initially:** Social media can be distracting and defeating if you get zero views. For your first 10-50 customers, step away from the internet. 
* **Go to physical places:** Find your target audience in the real world. If you're building a crypto wallet for teens, talk to parents at PTA meetings or farmers markets. Call your friends. Ask them what they think. 
* **Treat it as an exploration:** Work alongside humans. If a feature excites a specific person in real life, double down on it.

## 3. Charge Money on Day One
* **Free plans are noisy and useless in the early days.** You don't know if someone actually values your product if it's free.
* **Charge immediately:** Ask for money up front (e.g., $20/month). If they say no, that is valuable data—they will tell you *why*.
* **Pre-orders count:** Even if the product isn't fully ready, charge for a pre-order. You can always refund them if you don't build it. Real validation requires a credit card.

## 4. Scaling & Social Media (The Lottery)
* **Look for patterns:** Once you have paying customers, analyze *who* they are. If you notice a pattern (e.g., a lot of school teachers or video editors are using it), tailor all your online marketing to that specific niche.
* **Treat social media like a free lottery ticket:** If it won't ruin your mental health, post one video a day on TikTok/Instagram/YouTube. 
* **Focus on Inputs, not Outputs:** Do not stress over view counts (outputs). Celebrate the fact that you posted a video (inputs). If you stay consistent for 200 days, you will see results.
