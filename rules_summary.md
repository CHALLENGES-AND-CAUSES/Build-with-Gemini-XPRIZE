# Official Rules Summary: Build with Gemini XPRIZE

This document highlights the critical legal and operational constraints outlined in the official rules for the Build with Gemini XPRIZE.

## 1. Eligibility & Teams
* **Who can enter:** Individuals (of legal majority age), Teams, and Small Organizations (fewer than 25 employees).
* **Team Representation:** Teams and organizations must appoint one individual as the "Representative" to act on their behalf.
* **Multiple Submissions:** You can enter individually and be part of multiple teams. You can submit multiple projects, but **each submission must be unique and substantially different**.
* **Excluded Regions:** Residents of Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, and North Korea are prohibited from participating due to U.S. laws.

## 2. Project & Technical Constraints
* **New Projects Only:** Projects must be newly created *after* the hackathon start date (May 19, 2026). If you use pre-existing generic templates, frameworks, or boilerplates, you must explicitly explain how they were utilized.
* **Prior Support:** Projects cannot have been developed with prior financial or preferential support from XPRIZE or Devpost before the hackathon started.
* **Google Cloud:** You must use at least one product from Google Cloud.
* **LLM Usage:** If using LLMs, you **must use the Gemini API for at least one LLM call**. You are allowed to use other LLM providers alongside Gemini.

## 3. Submission & Testing Requirements
* **Language:** All submission materials must be in **English**. If the original is not in English, an English translation must be provided for the video, text, and testing instructions.
* **Video:** Must be less than 3 minutes long and show the project functioning on the device it was built for. Do not include copyrighted music or unauthorized third-party trademarks.
* **Testing Access:** You must provide free and unrestricted access to your working project for judging and testing. If your project is private, you must provide login credentials. (If it runs on proprietary hardware like wearables, you may be asked to provide physical access).

## 4. Intellectual Property
* **Ownership:** You (or your team/organization) retain full intellectual property ownership of your submission.
* **Open Source:** You may use open-source software/hardware provided you comply with their licenses and you build upon/enhance the underlying open-source product.
* **License to Sponsors:** By entering, you grant the sponsors a non-exclusive license to use the entry for judging and to promote the submission and use your likeness/name in promotional materials for up to three years.

## 5. Judging Process
* **Stage One (Pass/Fail):** Initial screening to ensure the project meets a baseline level of viability, fits the theme, and applies the required APIs/SDKs.
* **Stage Two:** Scored on the three main criteria: Business Viability, AI-Native Operations, and Category Impact.
* **Tie-Breaking:** If scores are tied, the winner is decided by the highest score in the first criterion (Business Viability). If still tied, the second criterion, and so on.
* **Verification:** Potential winners may be required to do a live call to demonstrate the project, answer questions, and provide additional financial documentation before the prize is confirmed.
