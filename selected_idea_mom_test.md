## Product definition (MVP for competition)

### Who pays
- Solo founders and early teams validating an idea  
- Product managers / UX researchers running discovery  
- Accelerator / community members doing customer interviews  

### What the business sells
A SaaS (or paid pilot) that:

1. **Plans** Mom Test–safe interview guides from a problem hypothesis  
2. **Reviews** transcripts (paste/upload) with Gemini  
3. **Flags** compliment-seeking questions, hypotheticals, and flattery answers  
4. **Extracts** assumptions, evidence quotes, and next actions  
5. **Stores** a living insight vault per project  

### What humans do vs what AI does

| Humans | AI (Gemini agents) |
|--------|---------------------|
| Pick the problem / customer segment | Build unbiased question sets |
| Run (or schedule) the interview | Score questions against Mom Test rules |
| Own final go/no-go call | Label evidence vs opinion in transcripts |
| Sell and support the product | Recommend next interviews and assumption status |

### Pricing (initial)
- **$29–49 / month** per founder seat, **or**  
- **$19–39 per interview pack** (e.g. 5 analyses) for faster first revenue  

Charge from day one; free-only plans are optional later, not for the competition window.
