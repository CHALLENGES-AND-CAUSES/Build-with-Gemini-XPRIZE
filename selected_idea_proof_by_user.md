## Product definition (MVP for competition)

### Who pays

- Solo founders and early teams validating an idea  
- PMs / UX researchers running discovery  
- Accelerator / community cohorts  

### What the business sells

Web SaaS that:

1. **Plans** Mom Test–safe interview guides from a hypothesis  
2. **Reviews** transcripts (paste/upload) with Gemini  
3. **Flags** biased questions, hypotheticals, flattery  
4. **Extracts** assumptions, evidence quotes, next actions  
5. **Stores** a living insight vault per project  
6. **Logs** agent decisions for product + competition evidence  

### Pricing (initial)

- **$29–49 / month** per founder seat, **or**  
- **$19–39 per interview pack**  

Charge from day one during the competition window.
